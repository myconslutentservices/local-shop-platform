Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

My Conslutent Services helps customers discover local businesses and helps business owners create and manage their business profiles.

Information we collect

When a business owner registers, we collect the business name, owner name, email address, contact numbers, business address, category, description, and details the owner chooses to provide. Account credentials are processed by our authentication provider; we do not display or store a plain-text password in the business profile.

How information is used

We use this information to create and manage owner accounts, display business profiles, provide platform features, protect the service, and respond to support requests.

Sharing and service providers

Information is processed by the hosting, database, and authentication providers needed to operate this website. We do not sell personal information. A business owner should only submit information they are authorized to share publicly.

Security and retention

We use access controls intended to limit business-management features to the relevant account. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain account and business data while needed to operate the service or meet legitimate operational and legal requirements.

Your choices and requests

For questions, correction requests, or account/data deletion requests, contact support@myconsultantservices.in. We may need to verify the request before acting.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
